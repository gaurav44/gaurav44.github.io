I'm a C++ software engineer with a background in computational science, high-performance computing, and engineering simulation.

My interests include modern C++, parallel programming, GPGPU, performance portability, and scientific software development.

I started as a mechanical engineer, and worked with numerical simulations before gradually moving deeper into software development. Today, most of my work revolves around C++ and performance-sensitive software.

## Education
### M.Sc. Computational Science and Engineering
**Technical University of Munich, Germany**

My master's work focused on scientific computing and high-performance computing. For my master's thesis, I ported a CFD solver to Kokkos for performance portability across CPU and GPU architectures.


### Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering
**Savitribai Phule Pune University, India**

My background in mechanical engineering is what originally introduced me to numerical simulation, CFD and scientific computing
